BIOGRAPHY::

Dr. Lukumba Phiri is a strategic academic leader and Senior Electrical Engineer with over 15 years of combined experience in higher education management and industrial turnkey projects. He currently serves as Senior Lecturer and Vice Chancellor at the University of Kabwe.

He holds a Ph.D. in Engineering with ICT Security (Smart Power Grid Security) from the University of Zambia and is currently a candidate for the Doctor of Engineering (DEng) at the Central University of Technology. His industrial background includes key engineering roles at ZESCO (Kafue Gorge Lower Hydropower Project) and SCAW Zambia Limited.

His research interests lie in Smart Power Grid Security, specifically False Data Injection Attacks, Cyber Risk Modeling, Digital Twins, Building Energy Performance, and Machine Learning applications in Smart Energy Systems. He is a registered professional engineer (PEIZ, REng) and a member of IEEE, ICTAZ, and ACEZ.
