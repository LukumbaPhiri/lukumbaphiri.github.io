01 · Profile

About

I am an engineer-scientist working on the security and trustworthiness of AI in critical infrastructure and cyber-physical systems. My research covers machine-learning detection and mitigation of cyberattacks on power-grid control systems, adversarial testbeds for industrial protocols, deepfake detection, NLP-based threat intelligence, and physics-informed modelling of building energy.

Before academia I spent more than ten years in power-sector and industrial engineering, most recently as Senior Electrical Engineer on ZESCO's Kafue Gorge Lower hydropower project, where I commissioned 13.8/330 kV generation protection and implemented IEC 61850 SCADA. That plant-floor experience shapes the questions I ask about grid security.

I have led institutions as Dean of Engineering and Vice-Chancellor, and I currently serve as Vice-Chancellor of the University of Kabwe. I was a technical committee member for Zambia's Smart Grid Regulatory Framework (Ministry of Energy, 2025).

02 · Research

Research themes

Four connected strands, from the substation control network to the building it supplies.

Theme A

Smart-grid cyber security

Detecting and mitigating false data injection attacks on state estimation and automatic generation control; cyber-risk modelling of Zambian ICS and SCADA.

FDIAAGCPetri netsReinforcement learning
Theme B

Industrial protocol testbeds

Virtualised testbeds for Modbus attacks, hardware-in-the-loop detection with process invariants, and SCADA versus IT traffic analysis.

Modbus/TCPIEC 61850HILSDN
Theme C

Digital twins & building energy

Hybrid data-driven and physics-based energy models for non-domestic buildings, validated across African climates; physics-informed neural networks for thermal-state recovery under sparse sensing.

PINNRC thermal modelsDigital twinsMPC
Theme D

Trustworthy & responsible AI

Deepfake audio detection in Zambian political speech and transformer-based extraction of emerging threats from a 4.06-million-post dark-web corpus.
